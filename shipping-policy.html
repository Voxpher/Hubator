Shipping Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2025

At Hubator, we are committed to delivering your products in a timely and reliable manner. Please take a moment to read our shipping policy to understand how we process and deliver your orders.

Estimated Delivery Time

Our standard shipping processing time is 1–2 days and delivery time is 5–7 business days from the date of order confirmation. However, in rare cases due to logistical issues, high demand, or unforeseen circumstances (such as weather, transport delays, or regional restrictions), delivery may take up to 10–15 business days.

We strive to ensure your order reaches you as quickly as possible, and you will always receive tracking updates once your product is shipped.

Delivery Options

  • Free Delivery — Standard shipping, delivered in 4–7 days.
  • Fast Delivery — Priority handling and quicker arrival in 3–4 days (+₹29).

Delivery times may vary slightly based on your location and courier availability.

Delivery Location and Availability

Shipping time may vary depending on your delivery location, product availability, and our shipping partner's reach in your area. Some remote or less accessible areas may experience slightly longer delivery timelines. If your pin code falls outside our regular serviceable zones, we will reach out to you before processing the order.

Order Cancellation After Shipping

Once an order has been shipped, cancellation is not possible. Please ensure all details (address, contact number, product choice, etc.) are correct at the time of placing your order. If you need to make changes before the item is shipped, please contact us immediately.

Incorrect Address or Failed Delivery

If the delivery fails due to an incorrect address or multiple unsuccessful delivery attempts by the courier, the package may be returned to us. Re-delivery charges will be borne by the customer and may add extra days to delivery.

Shipping Charges

Shipping charges will be clearly mentioned during checkout. Charges may vary based on destination, package weight, volume, and chosen shipping method.

Please note: Shipping charges are dynamic and may increase or decrease without prior notice, depending on courier rates, delivery zones, seasonal demand, or special conditions like remote area access. Some products or promotions may offer free shipping, which will be clearly stated on the product page or at checkout.
