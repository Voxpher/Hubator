Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

What is Hubator?

Hubator is a curated storefront for apparel, accessories, and everyday pieces from independent designers. We choose products for their materials, usefulness, and character.

What payment methods do you accept?

Available payment methods are shown securely at checkout. Depending on the order, these may include:

  • Credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover)
  • UPI and digital wallets (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, Apple Pay)
  • Net banking and bank transfers
  • Cash on Delivery (COD)

How can I contact you?

You can reach us via email at support@hubator.com for any inquiries, order assistance, or support. You can also send us a message on Instagram @hubatorofficial for quick responses.

How can I track my order?

You can track your order by logging into your account and going to the "Orders" section. Tracking details will be available within 48 hours after placing your order. We'll also send updates via email and WhatsApp once your package is shipped.

How do I return or exchange a product?

We do not accept returns. Replacement is only allowed within 5 days of delivery for valid reasons (wrong or damaged product). The item must be unused, in original condition, and with all tags intact.

Refunds are only issued if the product is unavailable or cancelled by Hubator. See our full Refund & Returns Policy for details.

Can I cancel or change my order after placing it?

Orders can be changed or cancelled within 3 hours of placing an order. After shipping, cancellation is not possible. Contact us quickly at support@hubator.com with your order ID or call +91 9242036989.

How long will delivery take?

Hubator offers two delivery choices:

  • Free Delivery — Standard shipping, delivered in 4–7 days.
  • Fast Delivery — Priority handling, delivered in 3–4 days (+₹29).

Delivery times may vary slightly based on your location and courier availability.

How can I get featured on the Hubator website?

Want to get featured on our website? Upload a video wearing Hubator products on Instagram and mention our handle @hubatorofficial in your caption!

What is Partial Payment on COD?

We have introduced a Partial Payment on Cash on Delivery (COD) option. Customers can confirm their order by paying just ₹99 in advance. The remaining amount can be paid in cash upon delivery.

This helps us reduce fake orders and ensures faster delivery for you. The ₹99 advance is a confirmation fee to secure your order.

Is the ₹99 COD advance refundable?

✅ Yes, if your order gets cancelled by Hubator or if the product is unavailable, your advance payment will be fully refunded.
❌ However, if you cancel the order after confirmation, the advance fee is non-refundable.

Can I order without the partial COD payment?

Yes. You can choose Prepaid Payment (UPI, NetBanking, Debit/Credit Card) and pay the full amount at once. But if you want COD, a ₹99 advance will be required.
