This Privacy Policy describes how Hubator ("Site", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your personal information when you visit, use our services, or make a purchase from hubator.com or otherwise communicate with us (collectively, the "Services").

By accessing or using our Services, you agree to the collection and use of your information as described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our Services.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Information We Collect

1. Information You Provide:

  • Contact info: name, address, email, phone number
  • Order details: billing/shipping info, order contents
  • Account details (if you create an account): username, password
  • Support messages: any info you send us through emails or forms

2. Automatically Collected (Usage Data):

  • Device/browser type and IP address

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience and track interactions with our site.

How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information to:

  • Process orders and fulfil deliveries
  • Provide customer support
  • Send account/order updates
  • Improve our website and services
  • Prevent fraud and ensure site security
  • Send promotional offers (if opted in)

Sharing Your Information

We may share your data with trusted third parties for order processing (e.g. payment gateways, delivery partners), customer service platforms, marketing services, and analytics tools. All third parties are required to respect your data privacy and security.

We do not sell or trade your personal information.

Cookies

We use cookies to save your preferences, track your activity, and analyse performance and usage. You can disable cookies via your browser settings. Some features may not work properly without them.

Payments

We do not store your payment card details. All payment transactions are securely processed through trusted third-party providers (such as Razorpay). These providers use encryption and follow industry standards to protect your information.

Marketing & Communication

If you sign up for our newsletter or promotions, we may send you marketing emails. You can opt-out at any time by clicking "unsubscribe" in our emails or by contacting us at support@hubator.com. Transactional emails (like order updates) will still be sent even if you opt out of marketing emails.

Data Retention

We retain your personal data as long as you have an account with us, for the time necessary to fulfil orders, and for legal and record-keeping purposes, including tax and accounting obligations.

Your Rights

Depending on your region, you may have the right to access, correct, download, or request deletion of your personal data, object to processing, withdraw consent, or request data portability. To exercise these rights, email us at support@hubator.com.

Security

We implement security measures to protect your personal data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and secure payment processing. No system is 100% secure — do not share your login credentials with others.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16. If you believe your child has shared information with us, contact us to have it removed.

Our site may contain links to other websites. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those sites.
