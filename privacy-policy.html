Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

This policy is a starting template for Hubator, not a substitute for legal advice. Before publishing, have it reviewed against India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and any other laws that apply to you, and fill in every bracketed placeholder with your real details.

1. Who we are

Hubator ("we," "our," "us") operates this website and sells physical products through it. This policy explains what personal data we collect when you browse, create an account, or place an order, and how we use it.

2. Information we collect

We collect information you give us directly, such as your name, email address, phone number, shipping and billing address, and order details, when you create an account, place an order, or contact us. We also collect payment information, but this is handled directly by our payment processor — we do not store your full card details on our own servers.

We automatically collect some technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, device information, and pages viewed, through cookies and similar technologies.

3. How we use your information

  • To process and fulfil your orders, including sharing your name, address, and order contents with our printing and fulfilment partner so they can produce and ship your item
  • To communicate with you about your order, account, or customer support requests
  • To process payments securely through our payment provider
  • To improve our website, products, and customer experience
  • To send you marketing communications, only if you have opted in, and you can unsubscribe at any time
  • To comply with legal obligations, such as tax and accounting requirements

4. Who we share your data with

We share the minimum information necessary with:

  • Our fulfilment partner (currently Qikink), to print and ship your order
  • Our payment processor, to securely process your payment
  • Our hosting and infrastructure providers, to run and secure this website
  • Analytics providers, if enabled, to understand site usage in aggregate

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

5. Cookies

We use cookies to keep you logged in, remember items in your cart, and understand how visitors use our site. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings, though some site features may not work correctly if you do.

6. Data retention

We retain order and account information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, and as required by tax, accounting, and other legal obligations.

7. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, download, or request deletion of your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and secure payment processing. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's privacy

Our services are not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent changes.

11. Contact us

For questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, contact us at [your support email] or by post at [your business address].
