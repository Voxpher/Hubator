Refund & Returns Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

This is a starting template, not legal advice. Since your products are fulfilled on-demand, adjust the specifics below (windows, exceptions) to match your actual fulfilment partner's policy before publishing.

1. Made-to-order products

Most items on Hubator are printed or produced specifically for you after you order. Because of this, we are only able to accept returns or exchanges in the situations described below — we generally cannot accept returns simply because of a change of mind, incorrect size chosen, or similar buyer's remorse, unless stated otherwise on the product page.

2. Damaged, defective, or incorrect items

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or different from what you ordered, contact us within 7 days of delivery at [your support email] with your order number and photos of the issue. We will arrange a free replacement or a full refund, at your choice.

3. Wrong size or fit

Please check the size guide on each product page carefully before ordering, as made-to-order items cannot always be exchanged for a different size. If an exchange is available for your product, it must be requested within 7 days of delivery, and the item must be unused, unwashed, and in its original condition.

4. How to request a refund or exchange

  1. Email [your support email] with your order number, the issue, and clear photos if the item is damaged or incorrect
  2. We will review your request and respond within 2 business days
  3. If approved, we will provide instructions for return shipping (if applicable) or process your replacement/refund directly

5. Refund processing

Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method and typically appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank or payment provider. Shipping charges are non-refundable except where the return is due to our error or a defective item.

6. Non-returnable items

  • Items marked as final sale on the product page
  • Custom or personalised items made to your specific request (e.g. custom text, uploaded designs), except in cases of defect or damage
  • Items returned outside the applicable window, or not in their original condition

7. Cancellations

Because production often begins shortly after an order is placed, cancellation requests can only be honoured if the item has not yet entered production. Contact us as soon as possible after ordering if you need to cancel.

8. Contact us

For any refund, return, or exchange questions, reach us at [your support email] or [your support phone number].
