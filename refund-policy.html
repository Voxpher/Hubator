Refund & Returns Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2025

1. Refund Policy

Customer satisfaction is our priority. However, please note that Hubator currently does not offer refunds once an order has been placed and delivered. Instead, we provide a Replacement Policy to ensure you are happy with your purchase.

2. Replacement Window

We do not accept returns. You have 5 days from the date of delivery to request a replacement. To be eligible, please ensure:

  • The item must be unworn and unused, maintaining its original condition
  • All original tags must still be attached to the item
  • The item must be returned in its original packaging
  • You must provide a receipt or proof of purchase

Important: For efficient processing of replacements related to damaged, incorrect, defective, or misprinted items, please provide clear photos and an unboxing video of the product at the time of delivery. These are crucial for our assessment.

⚠️ We only accept replacement requests if the item is damaged, incorrect, defective, or misprinted. Replacements for any other reason will not be processed.

3. How to Start a Replacement

To initiate a replacement, get in touch with our support team at support@hubator.com with your order number, clear unboxing photos and videos showing:

  • The damaged or defective product
  • The original packaging

4. Replacement Process

To ensure you receive your desired item quickly:

  1. Return the original item according to our return policy
  2. Once your return is accepted, you can place a new order for the correct item separately

Please note: if the reason for the replacement does not align with our policy, it may be declined. Approved replacement products will typically be delivered within 5–10 business days.

5. Order Cancellation

Orders can be changed or cancelled within 3 hours after placing an order. After shipping, cancellation is not possible. Contact us quickly at support@hubator.com with your order ID or call +91 9242036989.
